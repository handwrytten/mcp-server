# Handwrytten app data disclosure

This app-specific explanation follows the [Handwrytten Privacy Policy](https://www.handwrytten.com/privacy-policy/), reviewed September 19, 2026. The published policy controls; this disclosure adds no separate privacy commitments. Section 1 distinguishes Handwrytten's controller activities from processing on customers' instructions.

## Information used by the app

The app connects to a Handwrytten account through OAuth. Handwrytten handles sign-in and permission approval. Users should not enter passwords, API keys, authentication codes or payment-card numbers into ChatGPT tool arguments. Separate API-key authentication remains available for other MCP integrations.

To perform requested actions, the app processes account information, recipient and return postal addresses, handwritten messages, selected card and handwriting styles, signature images, uploaded artwork, basket contents and order information. Optional birthdays and anniversaries can be saved when users request occasion-based mailings; they are not required to send a card. QR-code features can store destination URLs and optional webhook URLs. Uploading artwork asks Handwrytten to fetch the supplied image URL.

Relevant information is exchanged between ChatGPT, the MCP server and Handwrytten's API. Tool results can include account, address and order information needed for the requested task. Preview images are produced for display. Known authentication fields are removed from successful JSON tool results; other personal information may remain.

Creating an address, uploading artwork or adding a basket item saves data in Handwrytten. Sending cards submits orders for physical production and mailing and may charge the payment method already configured in the Handwrytten account. Users must review the affected items and confirm before sending.

## Existing policy protections and choices

The policy describes service operation, fulfillment, payments, support, improvement, security and other permitted uses. It prohibits selling personal data and sharing order data for third parties' own marketing. It covers service providers, postal delivery, legal disclosures and business transfers (sections 3–5).

Section 7 generally retains account and order/transaction data for the account's duration plus three years, subject to legal exceptions; on-premises fulfillment data is retained for 1–2 business days.

Users worldwide can request deletion at contact@handwrytten.com using the subject “Data Deletion Request.” Section 9.3 describes verification, a 30-day response for deletion, and required-retention exceptions. Disconnecting the app does not itself delete Handwrytten records.

Consult the policy for complete rights, logging, cookies, transfers, security and contact details. This disclosure makes no separate claims about ChatGPT's data handling.
